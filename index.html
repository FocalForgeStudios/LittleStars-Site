Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Can I combine transit with enrichment sessions?

+

Yes - that's exactly how Little Stars works. Choose a core transit & after-school plan, then add Bible Study, Book Club or Homework Support sessions on top. Use the package builder above to see pricing in real time.

Whose car is used for the school run?

+

You choose. "School Run (Client Car)" uses your own family vehicle with our trained staff handling pick-up and drop-off. "School Run (Provider Car)" uses our vehicle for the full transit service.

What happens during the holidays?

+

Our Premium Holiday Programs keep your child engaged during school breaks - choose School Progress Study for academic revision, or the Enrichment Duo for Bible Study and Book Club together, on daily, weekly or monthly rates.

Is the Bible Study and spiritual content optional?

+

Completely. Home Bible Study is one module among several - Transit, Academic Support, Life Skills and Spiritual Growth are all selected independently, so your package reflects your family's preferences.

How do I get started?

+

Message us on WhatsApp or call either of our lines below. We'll talk through your child's school, schedule and needs, complete a short intake form, and confirm your final package and pricing.

Privacy Notice

How Little Stars handles personal information.

Little Stars Enrichment collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries, create parent accounts, manage child rosters, book sessions, record payments, and support parent-provider communication.

Information we collect

We may collect parent names, email addresses, phone numbers, child names, child ages, child care notes such as allergies or special needs, booking details, payment records, package requests, and chat messages sent through the website.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide childcare enrichment services, coordinate safe transit and sessions, communicate with parents, keep operational records, protect child safety, and comply with legal and accounting duties.

Children's information

Children's details are provided by a parent or guardian and are used only for care planning, scheduling, safety, and service delivery. We do not sell children's information or use it for behavioural advertising.

Sharing and storage

Data is stored through our service providers, including Supabase for authentication and database hosting. We share information only with authorised Little Stars staff and trusted service providers who help us operate the website and services.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information by contacting us at aminahdaisy141@gmail.com. We may need to keep limited records where required for safety, legal, or accounting reasons.

This notice is a practical website policy and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional for final compliance with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act and any other law that applies to your clients.
