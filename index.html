Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Can I combine transit with enrichment sessions?

+

Yes - that's exactly how Little Stars works. Choose a core transit & after-school plan, then add Bible Study, Book Club or Homework Support sessions on top. Use the package builder above to see pricing in real time.

Whose car is used for the school run?

+

You choose. "School Run (Client Car)" uses your own family vehicle with our trained staff handling pick-up and drop-off. "School Run (Provider Car)" uses our vehicle for the full transit service.

What happens during the holidays?

+

Our Premium Holiday Programs keep your child engaged during school breaks - choose School Progress Study for academic revision, or the Enrichment Duo for Bible Study and Book Club together, on daily, weekly or monthly rates.

Is the Bible Study and spiritual content optional?

+

Completely. Home Bible Study is one module among several - Transit, Academic Support, Life Skills and Spiritual Growth are all selected independently, so your package reflects your family's preferences.

How do I get started?

+

Message us on WhatsApp or call either of our lines below. We'll talk through your child's school, schedule and needs, complete a short intake form, and confirm your final package and pricing.
